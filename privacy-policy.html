Privacy Policy

PawLens · Last Updated September 27, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the PawLens developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use PawLens (the “App”).

When you start a breed scan or an AI Health Check, the photo you select is sent to a third-party AI image-analysis service to generate the result. Do not submit a photo containing information you do not want sent to that service.

2. Information handled by the App

Photos and camera input

You may take a photo with the in-app camera or choose an image from your photo library. The App lets you crop the photo, resizes it, and sends it to a third-party AI image-analysis service only when you start a breed scan or an AI Health Check. Photos may contain people, places, or other background details. Crop out information you do not want transmitted. Photos you add to a dog profile are kept on your device and are not uploaded.

Scan and health-check content

For a breed scan, the App sends the photo with a fixed question and receives the breed result. For an AI Health Check, the App also sends the body area, the signs you select, any note you type, and your dog’s breed and age if you have added them. The App stores the photo copy, the result, the time, and the collection or dog it belongs to locally on your device.

Dog profiles, care plans, records, and preferences

Dog names, breed, birth date, sex, weight, activity level, spay/neuter status, vaccine and vet records, weight entries, training progress, reminders, collections, favorites, onboarding state, free-feature usage counts, and related preferences are stored locally using Hive and SharedPreferences. Care plans and training recommendations are calculated on your device.

Reminders

Care reminders you schedule are created as local notifications on your device. They are not sent to us.

Technical, analytics, and diagnostic data

When the App contacts the AI image-analysis service, that provider may receive the image, the question, IP address, user-agent information, cookies, and ordinary request metadata. Firebase Analytics processes app-interaction events (such as screens, feature use, plan selections, and settings), Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, stack traces, and app/device information, and Firebase Remote Config retrieves app settings. The App does not attach photos, scan or health results, dog names, notes, or collection names to these events. See Firebase privacy information.

Advertising

Free users may see ads served by Google AdMob. On iOS, the App asks for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow it, your device’s advertising identifier may be used to show more relevant ads and measure ad performance; if you do not, ads are shown without it. PawLens Pro subscribers do not see ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Apple processes payments through your App Store account; we never receive your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases and unlock PawLens Pro. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

3. How information is used

  • provide photo-based breed identification and AI health guidance;
  • build care plans, training recommendations, and reminders on your device;
  • save and organize scans, dog profiles, and records on your device;
  • process and restore subscriptions;
  • show ads to users who do not subscribe;
  • remember settings and preferences;
  • diagnose failures and protect the service where reasonably necessary;
  • comply with law and enforce applicable terms.

4. Legal bases

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on your request to perform the service, consent for optional photo, camera, notification, and tracking access, legitimate interests in operating and securing the App, and compliance with legal obligations. The bases relied on may differ by territory.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We disclose scan and health-check inputs to our third-party AI image-analysis service provider to perform identification and health guidance. We disclose app-interaction and diagnostic data to Google Firebase, advertising data to Google AdMob for users who do not subscribe, and purchase data to RevenueCat and Apple. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in a corporate transaction subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Storage and retention

Saved scans, health checks, dog profiles, records, and collections remain on the device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App. Operating-system backups may preserve app data according to your device settings. Our service providers control how long they retain data they receive, as set out in their own privacy documentation.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Deny Camera, Photo Library, Notification, or Tracking access in iOS Settings; related features will be limited or unavailable.
  • Delete individual scans, health checks, dog profiles, records, or collections in the App.
  • Uninstall the App to remove its local container, subject to device backups.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings.
  • Contact us to exercise access, deletion, correction, objection, restriction, portability, appeal, or consent-withdrawal rights available in your region.

Because the App has no account or developer-operated cloud profile, we may be unable to identify or retrieve local-only data.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures. Images and requests are sent over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in their country), and we do not knowingly seek personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child submitted personal information should contact us.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Those transfers are governed by the safeguards each provider describes in its privacy documentation.

11. Safety and automated results

Breed results, mixed-breed percentages, AI health guidance, care plans, and feeding and exercise amounts are estimates only—not a veterinary diagnosis or professional advice. In an emergency, or if your dog seems unwell, contact a veterinarian immediately.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will update the “Last Updated” date and provide additional notice where required.
